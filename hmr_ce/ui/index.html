How HMR-CE Works (Plain-English Guide)

Hierarchical Multi-Resolution Context Engine with Surprise-Gated Memory

1. The Problem with Standard AI Memory

Standard AI models suffer from two big flaws:

  • They drown in chit-chat: Saving every mundane sentence clutters context and slows down thinking.
  • They hallucinate outdated facts: If you say "The meeting is at 2 PM" and later say "Actually, it's at 4 PM", normal vector databases retrieve both times and the AI gets confused!

2. Dual-Signal Surprise Radar (The Gatekeeper)

Just like your brain tunes out a ticking clock but snaps alert when someone drops a glass, HMR-CE measures two signals for every single sentence:

Topic Drift (S_topic)

Measures how far the subject has strayed from the current conversation topic.

Perplexity / Surprise (P_entropy)

Measures how unexpected or unusual the factual information is.

3. The 4 Memory Tiers

Tier 0: Active Buffer (K=6)

Short-term working memory for active banter.

Tier 1: Disk Vault (SQLite)

Permanent verbatim diary for exact numbers, code, and passwords.

Tier 2: Episodic Nodes (d=1024)

Deep conceptual memories tracking active vs. retracted beliefs.

Tier 3: Topic Centroids (d=64)

Macro summaries allowing the AI to scan years of memory in microseconds.

4. Self-Correcting Mind (Directed Belief Revision)

When you correct the AI (e.g. "Actually, our server IP is 10.0.8.99, not 10.0.4.12"), HMR-CE automatically marks the old fact as SUPERSEDED with a red strikethrough, draws an arrow to the new truth, and conceals the outdated fact from future prompts!
